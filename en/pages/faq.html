FAQ

How long does delivery take?

1–3 days in Cairo & Giza, up to 7 days for remote governorates.

Can I check the parcel before paying?

Yes — you can open and inspect the parcel before paying the courier.

How do I pick my size?

Check the size guide on each product page. Most of our pieces are oversized — size down for a closer fit.

How do I track my order?

Use the Track order page with your order number and phone, or your account if you're signed in.
